# SayNinja Digital Operations & Automation Case Study

A real-world case study showing how I translated owner conversations and business goals into a repeatable digital operating system for a three-location NYC kids fitness brand.

## What this project demonstrates

- Requirements discovery from stakeholder conversations
- Multi-location digital account architecture
- Workflow and role design
- Process simplification and product thinking
- Google Drive / Google Sheets / Apps Script automation
- Access-control and ownership decisions
- Measurement planning and iterative improvement

## Business context

SayNinja operates three NYC locations. The digital challenge was not simply to "post more." The business needed one recognizable brand, clear main-vs-local account rules, a practical way for location managers to supply daily footage, centralized ownership, and a system that could be measured and improved.

## From conversation to system

```text
Owner conversations
        ↓
Business requirements
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Account + workflow architecture
        ↓
Daily media intake system
        ↓
Automation + reporting
        ↓
Measurement + iteration
```

## Current operating model

```text
3 Gym Locations
      ↓
Gym Masters capture daily footage
      ↓
One upload folder per location
      ↓
Google Drive
      ↓
Apps Script daily check
      ↓
Google Sheets tracker
      ↓
Automated email summary
      ↓
Content selection / editing / publishing
      ↓
Performance review
```

The location-manager experience was intentionally simplified to:

**Film → Upload → Done**

The tracking and compliance work is handled centrally instead of asking location staff to rename files, manage subfolders, or maintain spreadsheets.

## Repository structure

- `docs/` — problem framing, architecture, decisions, and tradeoffs
- `automation/` — sanitized automation code
- `metrics/` — measurement plan and evidence to collect over time

## Current status

The digital operating system is being implemented in stages. The daily footage intake automation has been built and tested. Business-impact metrics are still being collected, so this repository intentionally avoids unsupported outcome claims.

## Privacy

This public case study is sanitized. It does **not** include child/customer data, private footage, credentials, internal Drive IDs, private email addresses, or confidential business information.

## What I am learning

This project is helping me practice the full path from an ambiguous business problem to a working system:

**Discovery → Requirements → Design → Implementation → Automation → Measurement → Improvement**
